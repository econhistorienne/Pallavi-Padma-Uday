## About me
Pallavi Padma-Uday is a business, economic and financial historian, with a particular focus on how culture shapes institutions, particularly the organisation of firms and finance in modern economies. Prior to her research career, she led digital audience engagement for New Corp companies in India, reported on political economy from South Asia for leading national newspapers, and mentored a tech startup backed by LSE Generate.
She is also a writer, backed by leading arts and culture organisations in the UK and Ireland. She was twice shortlisted for Arts Council Ireland's prestigious Literature bursary, and is a professional member of the Irish Writers Centre in Dublin. Her books have been acquired for the collections of the National Library of Ireland. 
In her literary work, she explores the dynamics of inequality and exclusion, themes that resonate with the informal institutions at the heart of her historical research.
